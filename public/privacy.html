Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2025

1. Overview

ScarsdaleBuzz ("we", "us", "our") provides a community-powered directory of local services. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

2. Information We Collect

  • Directory data: Business and contact information used for listings (e.g., names, phone, category, recommendation counts).
  • Usage data: Basic site interaction data (e.g., page views) to improve the experience. We do not sell personal information.

3. How We Use Information

  • To operate and improve the directory.
  • To display listings and categories to users.
  • To maintain quality and accuracy of listings.

4. Sharing

We may share aggregated, non-identifiable information for analytics or improvements. We do not sell or rent personal data. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.

5. Data Retention

We retain directory data as needed to operate the service. If you’d like a listing updated or removed, contact us using the information below.

6. Your Choices

  • You may request updates or deletion of a listing by contacting us.
  • For data access or deletion, see our Data Deletion page.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our directory is intended for general audiences and not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will post the updated version with a new “Last updated” date.
